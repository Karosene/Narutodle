Mechanics
    Booster cards
        - can be used by clicking on the card and choosing a character they would like to use the card on
        - they are one-time use cards that can help a player
        - player can hold only 5 booster cards at once
        - if player doesnt have space for another booster card, the game will automatically
        skip players move

        Heal - heals a chosen character for 25 health points (chance 30%)
        Chakra restore - restores 40 chakra to a chosen character (chance 25%)
        Jutsu recovery - cancels cooldown on all jutsus of a chosen character (chance 15%)
        Jutsu mastery - chosen character unlocks a special jutsu for 3 rounds (chance 15%)
        Jutsu damage boost - increases damage on all jutsus of a chosen character by 20% for 3 rounds(chance 5%)
        Splash damage - next attack will deal damage to 3 chosen characters at once for 1 round(chance 5%)
        Double attack - allows a player to use jutsus at once, they can be 2 jutsus from 2 different character 
                        but both jutsus are applied to the same character for 1 round(chance 5%)

    Charka system
        - chakra replenishes by 3 points per round when 7-8 characters are alive, by 5 when 5-6 chracters are alive,
          by 10 when 3-4 characters are alive, by 15 when 2 characters are alive and by 30 when 1 character is alive
        - chakra stop replenishing at 100

    Jutsu system
        - every jutsu has a 2 round cooldown
        - 2 base jutsus that can be used anytime, 1 special jutsu that can be used only when
          jutsu mastery card is active on that character
        - character that are attacked by someone with a certain attack type attribute of jutsu will be
          damaged less or more, depending on the users jutsu type attribute:
            Attack type Ninjutsu deals 10% more damage to Attack type Taijutsu characters
            Attack type Ninjutsu deals 10% less damage to Attack type Genjutsu characters

            Attack type Taijutsu deals 10% more damage to Attack type Genjutsu characters
            Attack type Taijutsu deals 10% more damage to Attack type Ninjutsu characters

            Attack type Genjutsu deals 10% more damage to Attack type Ninjutsu characters
            Attack type Genjutsu deals 10% more damage to Attack type Taijutsu characters

        - Jutsu effects
            - some effects will be used automatically like when chosen like: shield, damage supress or buff,
              but som jutsus need a character to be chosen heal(itself or team), posion/burn/bleed, stun, chakra drain
            heal - heals a chosen character for a cetain amount
            buff - increases characters attack power for a certain amount of rounds
            shield - when active, it cancels all of opponents attacks
            damage suppress - when active, it decreases power of opponents attacks

            poison/burn/bleed - damages a character across multiple rounds
            stun - disables the use of a character for centain amount of rounds
            chakra drain - deletes a certain amount of chakra from enemy character

Gameplay
    1. a coin is tossed to choose which player goes first
    2. players get 8 random cards from database, 1 of the characters has to be a character with passive ability
    3. players enter the game and first thing they do is take the booster card
    4. once theyve taken the card they can use it right away on likely character or save it for later
        - player can skip turn by clicking on skip turn button
    5. next they use jutsu by clicking on any character, their jutsus will pop up in a box
       with all 3 jutsu stacked bellow each other and player can choose what jutsu they want to use.
       then player chooses own/enemy character the jutsu will be used on, it depends on the type of jutsu
    6. once the player has used jutsu, its player 2s turn

    game ends when one of the players have no cards
