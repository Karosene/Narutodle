game starts by coinflip that decides who gets the first move

the move start by taking a booster card. then player chooses a character he wants to use jutsu with. 
he uses jutsu by clicking on chosen character and a little box pops up with all 3 jutsus. 
the upgraded jutsu will be disabled by default and only available through booster card "Jutsu Mastery"
then he chooses a character on who the player want the jutsu to be used. 
it can differ:
    heal - can be used on character himself or other players cards
    shield 0
