# Google AdSense Policy & Restrictions Compliance

## Understanding Policy vs Restrictions

**Google Publisher Policies (Violations = Account Risk)**
- Content that will NEVER be allowed to show ads
- Violations result in "Ad serving disabled" or account suspension
- Must be fixed immediately or ad code removed

**Google Publisher Restrictions (Advertiser Preferences = Reduced Revenue)**
- Content that is restricted from certain ad sources
- NOT a policy violation - won't suspend account
- Google Ads won't serve, but other sources might
- Results in "Restricted ad serving" with reduced revenue
- Optional to fix - can choose to monetize with limited ads

### Understanding Ad Serving Limits

**Important Distinction:**
- **"Limited ad serving"** = Limits the NUMBER of ads shown (account-level restriction)
- **"Limited ads (LTD)"** = Limits data collection for personalization (privacy feature, not a penalty)

**What Are Ad Serving Limits?**
Google may temporarily limit the number of ads your account can show for two main reasons:

1. **Account Being Assessed** (Normal for New Publishers)
   - Temporary limit while Google evaluates your traffic quality
   - Common for new AdSense accounts
   - Typically lasts less than 30 days (but can be longer)
   - Automatically reviewed and updated
   - NOT a violation - just an assessment period

2. **Invalid Traffic Concerns** (Action Required)
   - Limit due to suspicious or invalid traffic detected
   - Requires immediate attention to prevent account termination
   - Automatically reviewed as you improve traffic quality
   - Account remains accessible but with reduced ad serving

**What You Should Do:**

**If "Account Being Assessed":**
- ✅ Continue building quality content
- ✅ Grow your audience organically
- ✅ Ensure full policy compliance
- ✅ Be patient - Google is learning your traffic patterns
- ✅ Do NOT try to inflate traffic or clicks
- ⚠️ Limit will be lifted automatically (typically <30 days)

**If "Invalid Traffic Concerns":**
- 🚨 Review traffic sources immediately
- 🚨 Stop any paid traffic campaigns
- 🚨 Check for bot/spam traffic
- 🚨 Review [How Google prevents invalid traffic](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/16737)
- 🚨 Review [How to prevent invalid traffic](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7533706)
- 🚨 Learn to [segment your traffic](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/3123951)
- 🚨 Avoid [purchasing traffic](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1348737)
- ⚠️ Failure to fix may result in permanent account termination

**How to Prevent Invalid Traffic:**
- ❌ Never click your own ads
- ❌ Never ask others to click your ads
- ❌ Never buy traffic from shady sources
- ❌ Never use bots or automated traffic
- ❌ Never place ads on blank/error pages
- ✅ Only use organic, legitimate traffic
- ✅ Use Google Analytics to monitor traffic sources
- ✅ Block suspicious IPs if necessary
- ✅ Remove ad code from pages generating invalid clicks

### Limited Ads (LTD) - Privacy Feature

**What It Is:**
- A privacy-focused ad serving mode
- Disables collection, sharing, and use of personal data for ad personalization
- NOT a penalty or restriction
- Optional feature for privacy compliance (GDPR, COPPA, etc.)

**When to Use Limited Ads:**
- Content targeting children under 13 (COPPA requirement)
- Traffic from EU/EEA without user consent (GDPR)
- When you want to prioritize user privacy over ad personalization

**Impact:**
- Ads still serve, but may be less relevant (not personalized)
- Lower revenue per ad (non-personalized ads pay less)
- No personal data collected from users

**For Narutodle:**
- If targeting children: Enable limited ads for COPPA compliance
- If all-ages: Standard personalized ads are fine (with proper consent notices in Privacy page)
- Current setup: Privacy page already discloses ad personalization and opt-out options

**Key Difference Summary:**
| Aspect | Limited Ad Serving | Limited Ads (LTD) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **What it limits** | Number of ads shown | Data used for personalization |
| **Cause** | Traffic assessment or invalid traffic | Privacy settings/compliance |
| **Impact** | Fewer ads = less revenue | Less relevant ads = less revenue |
| **Status** | Account restriction (temporary) | Privacy feature (optional) |
| **Location** | Policy Center shows "Limited ad serving" | Ad settings, tag parameters |
| **Action needed** | Wait or fix invalid traffic | Choose based on audience/compliance |


### If You Receive a Policy Notification

**Notification Sources:**
- Email from AdSense
- Policy Center dashboard alert
- Example URL showing the violation
- Screenshots (sometimes) of the issue

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. **Read Notification Carefully**
   - Click "Fix" in the Action column for details
   - Review the example URL provided
   - Check screenshots if available
   - Note the deadline to fix

2. **Check Entire Site (Not Just Example)**
   - Don't assume it's only one page
   - Search for similar content across all pages
   - Review all pages that might have the same issue

3. **Fix the Issue**
   - **Option A**: Remove violating content
   - **Option B**: Remove ad code from violating pages
   - **Option C**: Change ad implementation if placement issue

4. **Request Review**
   - After fixing, click "Request Review" in Policy Center
   - Explain what you fixed
   - Wait for Google's response (3-7 days)

**Consequences of Not Fixing:**
- ⚠️ Ad serving disabled on affected site/pages
- ⚠️ Account suspension if multiple violations
- ⚠️ Loss of revenue during disabled period

**Common Violations to Watch For:**
- Accidental adult content in character images
- Too many ads vs content (more ads than game content)
- Ads placed too close to interactive buttons
- Blank pages with ad code
- Violating screenshot/image uploaded
